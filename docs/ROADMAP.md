# Research and product roadmap

1. Independently reviewed team-color benchmark: fixed splits, mixed-person/referee cases, error and abstention counts, no threshold tuning on validation.
2. Puck detection and temporal evidence: annotate visibility/occlusion, separate candidate detection from possession; evaluate on unseen clips.
3. Player identity: confirmed multiple views, same-team hard negatives, leakage controls and independent holdout; keep failed heads disabled.
4. Rink geometry: calibration and camera-motion handling before zones, distance or empty-ice claims.
5. Coaching rules: coach-reviewed episodes, explicit prerequisites, uncertain outcomes and conditional explanations; no blame without evidence.
6. Reproducible setup and review persistence: external asset checks, platform setup tests, reloadable jobs and dependency lock validation.
