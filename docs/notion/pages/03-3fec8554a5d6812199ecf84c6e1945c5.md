> Historical Notion snapshot. Design targets and older status claims are not current implementation guarantees. See [current baseline](../../BASELINE_QA.md). Child-identifying names, team name and individual profile details have been generalized for public sharing.

This is a **20-item annotated candidate library**, not a claim to have read 20 complete books. It intentionally includes 13 commercial books/annual drill books and 7 official developmental manuals; annual editions count as distinct resources but are not 7 independent theories. Selection is relevance-based, not a scientifically validated top-20 ranking. Bibliographic titles/authors for the first title need independent verification. Full-text analysis and chapter-level quotations require lawful access to the books. Source catalog and publisher previews support preliminary relevance assessments only.
