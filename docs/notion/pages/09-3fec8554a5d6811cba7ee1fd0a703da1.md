Replaces the earlier misleading count of 7 annual NHLCA books as seven independent theoretical contributions. This is a **bibliographic and thematic critical review of publisher metadata and accessible previews**, not a complete reading of 20 copyrighted books. B = publisher/catalog or Google Books description verified; C = candidate needing edition/authorship verification. A = full text/official original document read, none of these commercial books are A yet.

## Comparative judgment

For the selected player 8U, strongest immediate sources are Driver/Wharton, MacAdam/Reynolds, Cameron and official USA Hockey/Hockey Canada manuals. Johnston/Walter, Smith and Han should generate hypotheses about spatial options but should not set 8U requirements. Miller/Mack can inform parent tone but must not become a psychological diagnosis. Chambers drills are interventions rather than normative judgments. Historical conditioning works need modern pediatric sport-science verification.

## Literature gaps and quality controls

- Add peer-reviewed child development, autonomy-supportive coaching, sport specialization, deliberate play, and pediatric training sources as a separate evidence layer; these are not automatically hockey-specific.
- Distinguish tactical decision from technical execution and game outcome. Any rule must have observed preconditions, permitted alternatives, age-band, source, counterexample, uncertainty and drill.
- Rank evidence for child development: official youth pathways and original peer-reviewed research above adult tactical anecdotes.
- Reconcile USA Hockey 8U/10U local rule variants and [youth team name redacted] team instructions before enabling rule-based criticism.
- Claude audit: verify all 20 titles/authors/editions; flag weak C records and replace as appropriate; do not claim full reading.
