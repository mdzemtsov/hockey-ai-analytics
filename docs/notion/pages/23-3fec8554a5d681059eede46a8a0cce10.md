## Artifact produced

- Local generated file: /mnt/data/red\_player\_pilot\_[annotated.mp](http://annotated.mp)4 (4,420,909 bytes, 848×464, 424 frames, 14.118 s).
- Annotation: yellow circle around a *candidate* red skater approximately t=3–5s; title and timestamp overlay across clip.
- Implementation: OpenCV rendering using manually selected approximate points (t=3 x486 y308, t=4 x616 y293, t=5 x690 y287) and linear interpolation; not automatic object detection, identity tracking, or confirmed player path.
- Audio: this proof-of-concept MP4 was written using OpenCV VideoWriter and therefore does **not** preserve source audio.

## Quality and interpretation

- Rendering and file integrity verified by ffprobe; no external upload or public link created.
- Subject continuity is **unverified**. Circle may move off player between keyframes. It is a rendering demonstration only.
- No reliable puck possession or tactical decisions have been inferred. No coaching verdicts or individualized exercise recommendations.
- Real tracking acceptance requires sampled-frame identity review, error annotations, and manual QA. The full clip is too short for longitudinal conclusions.
