Build a chat-triggered, evidence-grounded hockey video analysis workflow for the selected player (8U [youth team name redacted], transitioning to 10U). The user places LiveBarn-permitted exports or GoPro video in Google Drive and writes “Вперёд. Новое видео.” Output: ten curated narrated annotated moments, source timecodes, full searchable event index, parent coaching report and development history. No separate user-facing app.

## Project status

Research and system design, version 0.1, 2026-10-18. No video pipeline has yet been deployed or validated. A Notion page is not executable software.

## Non-negotiable standards

- Analyze the full available recording, not merely ten preselected clips; choose up to ten pedagogically valuable events after processing.
- Never fabricate puck positions, jersey identity, camera coverage, passes, causal claims or timestamps.
- Explicitly mark tracking confidence, occlusion and missing context. If identification is uncertain, request confirmation or exclude the event.
- Separate observation, inference, recommended alternative, and actual outcome. A goal does not prove a good decision; a missed pass does not prove a bad one.
- Prefer age-appropriate skills, confidence, autonomy, fun and safe play over advanced NHL systems or goals scored.
- Every claim in an episode must be tied to exact video timestamps and visible evidence. Expert review before automatic release of critical technical conclusions.
- Avoid diagnosing psychological/medical conditions from video, body-shaming or public rankings of children.
- Privacy: parental control, restricted sharing, consent and venue/LiveBarn terms, retention/deletion policy.

## Documentation map

1. Research library: twenty candidate books/manuals, source verification and transferability.
2. Hockey ontology: age-banded decision rules, event definitions and coaching guardrails.
3. Technical pipeline: ingest, synchronize, track, interpret, annotate, narrate, QA and save.
4. Validation plan: ground truth, acceptance criteria, failure taxonomy and cost estimates.
5. Claude independent review: claims, citations, disagreements and test cases.

## Initial output specification

- 10 events by default: 4 strengths, 4 improvement opportunities, 2 tactical learning moments, adjusted if the game lacks defensible examples.
- A 6–10 minute parent review video with original footage, freeze-frame, arrows and Russian narration; optional 2–4 minute child-friendly highlight cut.
- Source-linked episode manifest (game time and source video time), complete game file retained, game summary and max 2–3 training priorities.
- If visual inference fails, deliver a transparent partial report, never a fake full analysis.
