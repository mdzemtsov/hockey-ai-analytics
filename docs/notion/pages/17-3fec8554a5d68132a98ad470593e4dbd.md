## Official free repositories (primary recommendation)

1. [Hockey Canada Drill Hub](https://www.hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/hockey-programs/drill-hub) — filters U7–U9, U11–U13 and categories Skating, Puck Control, Passing, Shooting, Small Area Game. Provides drill videos, diagrams and descriptions. Suitable starting point for the selected player; search by age before skill.
2. [Hockey Canada Player Development Downloads](https://www.hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/hockey-programs/players/essentials/downloads) — free plans, core skills, evaluation tools and diagrams; categories include skating, puck control, shooting, small-area games, off-ice.
3. [USA Hockey 8U/10U Practice Planner Guides](https://www.usahockey.com/practiceplannerguides) — downloadable age-specific plans and practice materials including dryland and small-area games; source is a guide index, not a guarantee of individual drill videos.
4. [Hockey Canada Initiation Practice Plan 20 (PDF with diagrams)](https://cdn.hockeycanada.ca/hockey-canada/Hockey-Programs/Drill-Hub/Downloads/Practice-Plans/hc-initiation-practice-plan-20-e.pdf) — pairs passing, circle keep-away, bank passes and skating, directly printable diagrams.

## Individual illustrated drills with embedded video

1. [Neutral Zone Support 2 on 1 — Ice Hockey Systems](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/neutral-zone-support-2-1) — diagram + embedded Vimeo 184518103; support spacing and 2v1. Links ICE-IQ-01, OF01, OF02. Full drill advanced; simplify to half-ice and one defender for 8U.
2. [Quick 2 vs 1 Attack](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/quick-2-vs-1-attack-situational-drill) — diagram + Vimeo 346606086; attack/defense read after turnover. Links ICE-PU-03, ICE-IQ-02, PU03, TR01. Adapt speed and space.
3. [Rondo Circle Passing to 2 on 1](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/rondo-circle-passing-2-1) — diagram + Vimeo 601021977; puck support, passing, awareness, transitions. Links ICE-IQ-01, ICE-IQ-02, OF01, TR01. Reduce numbers/pressure for 8U.
4. [Puck Support in 2 v 1 Situations — practice plan](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/practice/1/3048921) — multiple diagrams and embedded drill video links; station format and lower-age variation, including Decision Time 2 on 1. Links ICE-IQ-01, ICE-PU-03.
5. [2 v 2 Tire Pass Game With Support](https://ihsdev.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/2-v-2-tire-pass-game-support) — diagram; pass-and-move support. Video presence on page not guaranteed. Links ICE-IQ-01, OF01, PU05.
6. [2 v 2 Play Fast With Support](https://ihsdev.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/2-v-2-play-fast-support) — diagram + Vimeo 1099383595, pro development camp example; transition and support. Links ICE-IQ-02, TR01, TR02. Not an 8U-ready unmodified practice.
7. [Sabre Split Ice Attack](https://icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/sabre-split-ice-attack-drill) — diagram + Vimeo 1103984088; professional development camp, passing and 2v1. Links ICE-PU-03, OF08. Reference only for coaches, not direct 8U drill.

A publicly accessible webpage does not establish re-use rights. For a first-party exercise illustration, make a new original schematic rather than copying publisher diagrams.
