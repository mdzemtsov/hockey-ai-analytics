> Historical Notion snapshot. Design targets and older status claims are not current implementation guarantees. See [current baseline](../../BASELINE_QA.md).

Independent red-team review of Hockey AI Analytics v0.1. Do not accept model claims on authority. Distinguish sourced facts, proposals and untested capabilities.

## Source audit

- Check every bibliographic author, title, edition, date, ISBN and whether the actual text was accessed. Identify books versus manuals and multiple annual editions.
- Check official U9/U11/8U/10U age guidance and whether practice percentages were improperly treated as game score weights.
- Find conflicting youth development guidance, differences between USA Hockey and Hockey Canada, and relevant competition rules.

## Engineering audit

- Is identity tracking feasible in LiveBarn footage with low resolution and overlapping players? What is the measured precision and recall?
- Is puck detection reliable? Are the game clock and camera timestamps aligned? Can rink homography handle pan/zoom?
- Does a VLM actually observe the claimed events? Are outputs anchored to verified frames?
- Can the chosen execution environment read large videos, run GPU inference, render MP4, write Drive, and trigger via chat?
- Can we avoid a separate user-facing app without pretending backend compute is unnecessary?
- Are video rights, parental privacy, access controls and retention addressed?

## Coaching audit

- Would a qualified youth coach agree with each alternative? Are suggestions age-appropriate, non-shaming and consistent with team instructions?
- Test cases: missed pass with good decision; goal after bad decision; hidden puck; similar jersey; player occlusion; reverse attacking direction; half-ice match; no discernible useful clips.

## Deliverable

Issue-by-issue table: claim, evidence, severity, reproduction/test, proposed fix, confidence, owner. Separate must-fix blockers from optional improvements.
