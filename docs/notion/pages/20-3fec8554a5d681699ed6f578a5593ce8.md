## Record hierarchy

Season → Game → Verified Episode → Opportunity → Coaching Interpretation → Priority → Exercise Plan → Optional Practice Feedback → Next Comparable Game Review.

Every entry has date, author/reviewer, source, version and uncertainty. Raw clips are linked from restricted Drive, not copied into public Notion.

## Tables (proposed schema)

Game: game\_id, date, team, age\_band, game\_format, opponent\_level\_optional, venue\_optional, camera\_type, video\_uri\_private, rights\_status, reviewer, coverage\_ratio.

Episode: episode\_id, game\_id, start/end, player\_confirmed, puck\_visibility, phase, rule\_id, observable\_facts, interpretation, decision\_rating\_or\_NA, execution\_rating\_or\_NA, outcome, alternatives, coach\_signoff.

Opportunity: opportunity\_id, game\_id, skill\_rule, eligible?, visibility, contextual\_factors, adjudication.

Priority: priority\_id, theme, source\_episode\_ids, first\_seen, status, reason, coach\_note, next\_review.

ExerciseSession: plan\_id, exercise\_id, optional date, completed?, enjoyment?, difficulty?, modification, parent\_note.

Trend: rule\_id, comparable\_games, numerator, denominator, coverage, source\_video\_quality, reviewer, uncertainty, narrative, no causal claim.

## Evaluation

- Never compare raw counts when one game has 20 minutes of usable footage and another has 50.
- Do not compare across rink formats or opponent levels without a context note.
- No child ranking, mental diagnosis, permanent talent score, or public leaderboard.
- Report observed improvement as 'more frequently made himself available in 6 of 8 clearly visible opportunities versus 3 of 7' only when actual reviewed evidence supports those numbers.
- If data are sparse, report 'insufficient comparable observations'.

## Parent-facing review

After each game: 2 strengths, at most 2 development themes, 1–2 optional activities, 1 positive discussion question. Monthly: compare similar opportunities, celebrate process and enjoyment. Seasonal: skills coverage, development trajectory, motivation, workload and rest.

## Child-facing view

A 2–4 minute optional highlight reel, one 'superpower' to celebrate, one fun mission, and no percentage grades. Child can opt out of watching analysis.

## Data governance

Private by default. No automatic exposure of other children's identities. Export/delete workflows must be defined before production. Human review required for sensitive or negative feedback.

## Open questions

Who is the primary human reviewer? How much game footage is realistically available? Does the team permit filming and sharing? What retention period does the family prefer? These do not block design but block production decisions.
