## Source classes

## Verified primary references

1. [Hockey Canada U9 age division](https://www.hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/hockey-programs/players/essentials/age-divisions/under-9): ages seven/eight; fun and fundamental skills; practice allocation 75% technical / 15% individual tactics / 10% team tactics / 0% team play / 0% strategy. **Do not use these as game evaluation weights.**
2. [Hockey Canada U11 age division](https://video.hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/hockey-programs/players/essentials/age-divisions/under-11): ages nine/ten; fun, refine skills, introduce team tactics; practice 50/20/15/10/5 (technical / individual tactics / team tactics / team play / strategy).
3. [Hockey Canada U11 coaching skills](https://hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/hockey-programs/coaching/under-11/coaches/skills): U11 Learn to Play; 85% skills and tactics, 15% team play and systems; position rotation LW/C/RW/RD/LD over five games recommended early in season.
4. [Hockey Canada player downloads](https://www.hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/hockey-programs/players/essentials/downloads): U9/U11 full and abridged player pathways, core skills, practice plans, skill evaluations.
5. [Hockey Canada coaching downloads](https://hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/hockey-programs/coaching/essentials/downloads): U9 skill matrix, phase practice plans and skill evaluation manual.
6. [USA Hockey Practice Planner Guides](https://www.usahockey.com/practiceplannerguides): separate 8U/10U/12U/14U planners, practice planning, small-area games, off-ice and long-term development.
7. [USA Hockey Training Materials](https://www.usahockey.com/trainingmaterials): age-specific modules, small-area games, goaltending and contact resources.
8. [Hockey Plays and Strategies, second edition](https://us.humankinetics.com/products/hockey-plays-and-strategies-2nd-edition-epub): Mike Johnston and Ryan Walter; Human Kinetics, 2019, 248 pages, ISBN 9781492570363; publisher states >160 plays and systems. Use for advanced tactical alternative hypotheses, not normative U9 scoring.
9. [Multi Player Tracking in Ice Hockey with Homographic Projections](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13397): Prakash et al., 2024. Ice-hockey-specific player tracking research; homographic projection of foot keypoints improves identity association under occlusion. Need independent reproducibility check on GoPro/LiveBarn youth footage.
10. [Ice hockey puck tracking through broadcast video](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925231223006070): Neurocomputing 2023, DOI 10.1016/j.neucom.2023.126484. Research documents difficulty with high-speed low-texture near-invisible pucks; no basis to claim generic detector solves it.

## Important reconciliation

The U9 75/15/10 and U11 50/20/15/10/5 splits are **practice design recommendations**, not a numerical formula for grading an individual player's game. U11 positional rotation matters: do not assume the selected player is permanently left wing or penalize role differences without coach instructions. A hockey player tracker preserving a track ID is not the same as identifying a specific child. A narrated video must be traceable to source footage.
