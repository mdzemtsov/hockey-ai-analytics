# 🔁 15 — Closed-loop training recommender and weekly workflow

[Notion source](https://app.notion.com/p/3fec8554a5d68139bc20e35b6a896e29?pvs=204) · [Index](../README.md)

> Historical Notion snapshot. Design targets and older status claims are not current implementation guarantees. See [current baseline](../../BASELINE_QA.md). Child-identifying names, team name and individual profile details have been generalized for public sharing.

## Closed-loop process

1. Input: new game, player age/format, weekly team load, parent availability, fatigue, child preferences.
2. Video QA: confirm the selected player identity, puck visibility, context and coaching rule applicability; abstain if uncertain.
3. Extract opportunities: rank by coach-reviewed importance, repeat frequency, improvement potential and child-friendly actionability. Do not rank by goals alone.
4. Choose at most two priorities for the next 1–2 weeks; keep at least one existing strength for positive reinforcement.
5. Match each priority to 1 on-ice and 1 off-ice exercise where feasible, plus optional game-IQ and play variation. If no extra ice, select off-ice or a question to ask the existing coach.
6. Fit to actual schedule and fatigue; preserve rest days, family time and unstructured play. Never stack high-intensity work after hard practices without assessment.
7. Parent gets 10-minute optional micro-session card and a note for team/private coach. Child gets fun invitation, not a performance deficit report.
8. At next game compare eligible visible opportunities, camera coverage, context and reviewer agreement; track trend with uncertainty, not simplistic percentage improvement.
9. Log completion as optional yes/no + enjoyment 1–5 and perceived difficulty 1–5, not surveillance or pressure.
10. Update rule/exercise efficacy only after multiple reviewed cases; avoid claiming AI learned from one game.

## Recommendation scoring (heuristic, not scientific model)

Priority = clear video evidence + frequent eligible opportunity + age-appropriate coach importance + trainability - uncertainty - excessive load. Hard constraints: safety, no missing identity, no unsupported tactical claim, parental permissions and player enjoyment.

## Sample micro-sessions (choose ONE, not all)

A. 8–10 min off-ice: 2 min free ball handling, 3 x 45s soft-ball maze with breaks, 3 x 45s wall pass and move with breaks, 2 min freestyle challenge.

B. 8–10 min athletic: 2 min warmup play, 2 x 3min animal movement with rest, 2 x 5 low jump-and-stick, finish with fun race.

C. 5–7 min game IQ: watch 3 short permitted clips, pause before action, child offers options, adult praises reasoning not result.

D. On-ice coach note: ask for 2v1 moving support or continuous 2v2 switch inside normal team practice; no extra rink booking assumed.

## Example feedback card

Observed at 06:21–06:29 (illustrative only, not real video): the selected player was visible behind the defender while teammate had puck. Decision evidence: passing lane unavailable; confidence pending review. Training priority: create a passing lane by moving feet. Suggested ICE-IQ-01 2v1 moving support or OFF-ST-03 decision gates. Recheck: count only clear support opportunities next game, not number of goals.

## Evaluation and anti-overtraining controls

Weekly context includes number of practices, games, other sports, sleep/fatigue and child choice. Additional structured drills are optional and should be omitted during busy tournament weeks or pain. Avoid specializing every available hour in hockey at age 8. No generic recommendation for maximal strength loading or high-volume repetitive shooting. Pain or injury prompts rest and appropriate assessment, not automatic training adjustment.

## Data model

SessionPlan: session\_id, game\_id, priority\_rule\_ids, exercise\_ids, date\_range, time\_budget, existing\_practice\_load, child\_preference, safety\_flags, status.

SessionLog: completed?, enjoyment?, difficulty?, parent note, coach note; no punitive streaks.

ProgressReview: comparable\_game\_ids, eligible opportunities, coverage, rule-version, reviewer, observed trend, confidence, adjustments.

ExerciseFeedback: exercise\_id, coach rating, child enjoyment, accessibility, modification, incident, source and version. Never train model on identifiable minor footage without documented permission.

## Delivery channels

- Notion: authoritative library, decisions, change log and progress record.
- ChatGPT: command-driven plan and short parent-friendly summary.
- Google Drive: video and media outputs when integration is implemented.
- Coach: printable one-page session note or link, only if parent chooses.

## Release gates

No automatic training recommendations from unreviewed video interpretations; coach-approved age-safe rules, valid exercise instructions, privacy controls and parent override required. The recommender is specified here, not yet implemented.
