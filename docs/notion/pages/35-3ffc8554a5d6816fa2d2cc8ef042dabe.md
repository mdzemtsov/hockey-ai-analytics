# Hockey AI Analytics — V12 team and learned identity checks: rejected after testing

[Notion source](https://app.notion.com/p/3ffc8554a5d6816fa2d2cc8ef042dabe?pvs=204) · [Index](../README.md)

> Historical Notion snapshot. Design targets and older status claims are not current implementation guarantees. See [current baseline](../../BASELINE_QA.md).

Implemented explicit two-team upper-jersey evidence plus a frozen DINOv2 visual descriptor comparing a selected player against first-frame competitors. Tested three variants: unchanged V11, team only, and team plus identity. The model is a general image feature extractor, not hockey-trained identity recognition.

## Controlled comparison

V11 replay reproduced all earlier development metrics exactly. On the consumed CNTRL–PAC interval, dark-target wrong-person frames fell from 48 to zero, but correct coverage fell from 80/184 to 32/184 with team checks and 12/184 with the identity gate. This is excessive abstention, not success.

## New temporal validation

Candidate source, configuration and model weights were locked before downloading CAR–NYR frames 1801–2400: all 600 frames, 10 seconds, two first-frame visual selections. No future GT entered inference.

Blue target: V11 correct 111/600 with 364 wrong-person frames; team-only 110/600 with 361 wrong-person frames; full identity 9/600 with zero wrong-person frames. White target: V11 and team-only 159/162 correct; full identity 18/162. All methods had zero outputs while reference targets were absent.

Decision: reject both new variants for deployment. Team evidence cannot distinguish teammates. The single-view learned identity gate rejects too many correct observations. Visual spot review confirms a teammate substitution. This is temporal continuation of another previously studied match, not independent-match validation; publisher labels still await independent human review.

Ten focused tests passed. Four full three-way comparison videos decoded correctly. Sources, pinned model/weights, configuration, state/coordinate CSVs, evidence reasons and QA are saved locally in hockey\_ai\_analytics/v12. No raw videos or coordinates uploaded to Notion. Main V6 application unchanged; automation remains paused. Test players are not identified as Matvey.

Next experiment: hockey-specific multiview identity evidence, reviewed identity data, same-team hard negatives, and manually confirmed gallery additions. Unverified reconnections must never teach the identity model. No readiness claim.
