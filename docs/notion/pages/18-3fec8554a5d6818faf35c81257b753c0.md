## Editorial standards

1. No false 'watch video' label for category hub or text-only guide. 2. Keep direct source landing URL, access status and verification date. 3. Video playback verification is separate from page existence. 4. Do not reproduce copyrighted diagrams or videos without permission. 5. For original exercise cards, create independently drawn schematics and short demo videos with consent. 6. When age fit is uncertain, mark coach review required. 7. Treat injury/recovery entries as educational, not demonstrations.
