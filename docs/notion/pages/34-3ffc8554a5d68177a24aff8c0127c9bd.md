Implemented a causal selected-player recovery layer with immutable first-frame appearance, motion and size checks, ambiguity/overlap abstention, three-frame reconnection confirmation and expiry requiring explicit reselection. It remains experimental; V6 application unchanged.

## Results

Seven selected Chicago–Toronto development cases: correct visible coverage 98/278 to 186/278; measured wrong-person frames 88 to 0. These are correlated, deliberately selected failure cases, not general accuracy.

Frozen candidate tested on all 600 frames of a reserved 20-second CNTRL–PAC continuation, with two first-frame visual selections and no future reference in inference. Dark target: correct 74/184 to 80/184; wrong-person frames 94 to 48. Light target: correct 59/357 to 56/357; wrong-person frames 0 for both. Candidate rejected for deployment.

Spot visual inspection confirmed an opponent was selected during one recovery: color-histogram and motion agreement did not prove identity. Thirteen unit tests passed; nine comparison videos were fully decoded and frame counts verified. Publisher labels remain unmodified; independent human verification pending. This is temporal validation from a previously studied match, not an independent-match test.
