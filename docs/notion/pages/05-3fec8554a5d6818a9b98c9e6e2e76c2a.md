These are design hypotheses for expert review, not machine-validated ground truth. Every card needs age-specific adaptation and source links before being activated. Rule engine must emit 'insufficient evidence' when footage does not support a judgment.

## OFF-01 — Puck carrier under pressure

- Detectable context: Identify puck carrier and approaching defender; estimate available passing lanes.
- Candidate preferred choices: Carry into space, pass to available support, protect and turn away.
- Evidence / caution: Do not demand a pass if no reliable teammate/puck view.
- Practice mapping: 2v1 keep-away, 3v3 small-area.
- Rating axes: decision, execution, outcome separately; require source timestamps, team instructions and identity confidence.

## OFF-02 — Support behind puck

- Detectable context: Teammate carries into zone; the selected player off puck.
- Candidate preferred choices: Offer diagonal passing lane, stagger depth, stay available.
- Evidence / caution: Avoid arbitrary 2m spacing rule; contextual.
- Practice mapping: 3v2 support triangles.
- Rating axes: decision, execution, outcome separately; require source timestamps, team instructions and identity confidence.

## OFF-03 — Open ice receiving

- Detectable context: the selected player anticipates pass.
- Candidate preferred choices: Present stick target, move to space, receive in stride.
- Evidence / caution: Never claim he looked without clear evidence.
- Practice mapping: Pass-and-follow moving lanes.

## OFF-04 — Shot vs pass

- Detectable context: the selected player in shooting range.

- Evidence / caution: Outcome-independent; do not assume shot was bad because saved.
