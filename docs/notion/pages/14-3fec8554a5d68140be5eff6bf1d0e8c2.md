# 🔐 13 — Youth video privacy, rights and child-safe feedback

> Historical Notion snapshot. Design targets and older status claims are not current implementation guarantees. See [current baseline](../../BASELINE_QA.md). Child-identifying names, team name and individual profile details have been generalized for public sharing.

## Data handling

Video includes minors beyond the selected player; treat as restricted. Keep access private by default, do not share raw footage or public links, avoid face recognition if jersey/shift/parent confirmation suffices, minimize names of other children, restrict retention and deletion, and document processor/subprocessor policies before uploading to any new service. Avoid biometric identification claims.

## Legal/contractual gates

Recording permission and venue rules are separate from platform terms. For LiveBarn, use only exports explicitly authorized by account terms and venue rules. Do not bypass access controls, download restrictions or DRM. Parental authority over the selected player does not automatically grant permission to publish other minors' images.

## Coaching voice

Praise precise observed behavior, not innate talent. Do not shame, rank against teammates, infer confidence/anxiety or diagnose from expressions. Present one learning point per clip, an alternative, and an optional playful mini-game. If the footage is inconclusive, say so. Parents receive nuanced analysis; child's version is shorter, upbeat, and not a report card.

## Escalation

If footage suggests a possible dangerous collision or injury, do not diagnose or use AI to determine return-to-play; advise appropriate real-world professional assessment. If child requests not to be filmed or shown a critique, respect family preferences and avoid child-facing release.

## Privacy release checklist

1 rights and permissions confirmed; 2 source restricted; 3 access list explicit; 4 model/vendor use policy checked; 5 child-safe narration review; 6 metadata strips unnecessary personal details; 7 deletion path documented; 8 sharing links restricted; 9 consent withdrawal workflow; 10 audit log preserved.
