Date 2026-10-19. Source same user-uploaded 14.118-second MOV. User instructed to achieve real technical progress rather than documentation.

## Executed experiment

- Ran OpenCV HSV red-color segmentation and connected-component grouping on **all 424 frames** (30.03fps).
- Wrote /mnt/data/red\_team\_detection\_[v2.mp](http://v2.mp)4 (14.118 seconds, 5,209,117 bytes) and /mnt/data/red\_detection\_metrics.json.
- Mean 10.66 candidate red blobs/frame, range 9–12. These are **blobs, not verified player counts**.
- Nearest-neighbor green marker was assigned in 364 frames (12.12 sec) after initialization; this is NOT a verified identity track and does not establish 12.12 seconds of correct tracking.

## Grounded failure

- Manual inspection at \~4.03 sec confirms red advertising/signage above the ice falsely detected as red players.
- Connected components can fragment one jersey or combine nearby players.
- Nearest-neighbor can silently switch between players. The metric is not accuracy.
- No puck detection, possession tracking or defensible individual tactical critique achieved.

## Decision

**Baseline failed identity/reliability gate. Do not use this for coaching recommendations.** Do not conflate rendered green marker with confirmed track.

## Next experiment

- First segment actual rink/ice mask by image geometry and brightness, excluding boards, spectators, ads and bench.
- Detect full-body player boxes using an object detection model, then classify jersey color inside torso ROI (not raw red pixels alone).
- Associate tracks with motion prediction and occlusion state; manually review ground truth at 1–2 fps and around crossings.
- Report precision/recall, false positives and ID switches against labeled frames, and abstain when identity uncertain.
- Only after identity passes: manual puck/decision observation and coaching review.
