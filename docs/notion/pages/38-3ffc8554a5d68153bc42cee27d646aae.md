# Coaching review prototype — pauses, graphics and bilingual voice

[Notion source](https://app.notion.com/p/3ffc8554a5d68153bc42cee27d646aae?pvs=204) · [Index](../README.md)

> Historical Notion snapshot. Design targets and older status claims are not current implementation guarantees. See [current baseline](../../BASELINE_QA.md).

Implemented local interval selection (up to 30 seconds), team and explicit player-frame modes, player/goalie/referee/goal/puck detection candidates, conservative jersey-color hypotheses, pause-point graphics, conditional passing-lane rules, English/Russian transcripts and locally generated audio. JSON/CSV exports and source/model/code fingerprints are retained locally.

24 unit/replay tests passed. Repeated service tests in both modes generated and served English/Russian audio; representative files decoded with non-silent signal. Browser verified automatic pause, graphic frame, language switch, voice controls, continuation and saved-review reopening. Selected-player mode was tested with an actual bound selection and remains UNKNOWN at other sampled frames.

Real-clip outcomes: original test segment had two-color evidence in 1/7 samples and single puck candidates in 3/7. Chicago–Toronto had 0/4 two-color samples and 2/4 puck candidates; Carolina–Boston had 0/4 and 0/4. These are output counts, NOT ground-truth accuracy. All real clips abstained from conditional arrows. The arrow rule passed controlled fixtures only.

Team recognition, continuous puck tracking, possession, zones, tactical roles, empty-ice understanding and correct/incorrect decision grading are NOT validated. The prototype is ready to jointly test the interaction and narration, not to trust as an autonomous coach. V13/V14 failed identity heads remain disabled. No raw videos, images or coordinates uploaded. Test player is not Matvey; automation remains paused.

Next accuracy work: independently confirmed team/puck labels, measured precision and coverage, rink calibration and temporal possession evidence, followed by coach-reviewed tactical episodes.
