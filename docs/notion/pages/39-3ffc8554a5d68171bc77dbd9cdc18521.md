# V15 — team-color audit and passing-lane safeguards

[Notion source](https://app.notion.com/p/3ffc8554a5d68171bc77dbd9cdc18521?pvs=204) · [Index](../README.md)

> Historical Notion snapshot. Design targets and older status claims are not current implementation guarantees. See [current baseline](../../BASELINE_QA.md).

Audited 51 deterministic crops from three existing clips. Labels are assistant visual review, NOT independent human ground truth. First sampled frames used for development, last sampled frames for validation; matches overlap across splits.

A broader torso-color classifier was frozen before validation and rejected: among 24 eligible validation crops, correct labels increased 7→12, wrong labels increased 3→5, and UNKNOWN fell 14→7. Mixed-person crop assignments increased 1/3→3/3. More labels did not establish safer recognition. Existing color classifier remains unchanged.

Integrated coaching safeguards: unknown-color players, goalies and referees now block candidate passing lanes; explicitly requested team color cannot silently fall back to another team; a third visible color prevents a two-team hypothesis. 27 unit/replay tests pass. No claim that these changes establish possession, correct game decisions, or safe passes. Updated local build uses port 8770.

Full local evidence retained in v15/[QA.md](http://QA.md), crop manifest, fixed labels, classifier fingerprint, comparison and summary. No images, raw video or coordinates uploaded. Test player is not Matvey. Automation remains paused.
