# Hockey AI Analytics — V13/V14 hockey-specific identity training and dataset review

[Notion source](https://app.notion.com/p/3ffc8554a5d68176badbc08e785fd657?pvs=204) · [Index](../README.md)

> Historical Notion snapshot. Design targets and older status claims are not current implementation guarantees. See [current baseline](../../BASELINE_QA.md).

20 October 2026. Trained two 131,456-parameter metric heads over a frozen DINOv2 encoder, with supervised contrastive learning and explicit same-team hard negatives. These are learned hockey-specific weights, not just another untouched general model; the backbone was not fine-tuned.

V13: 16 training tracks / 342 real crops, six held-out calibration tracks. V14 expanded to 28 training tracks / 1,413 crops, plus six calibration tracks / 346 crops, spanning 50 seconds from each of three training matches. Enrollment for eight Chicago–Toronto identities used up to 12 actual earlier views (89 total).

## Data audit

Assistant sampled review excluded goalies, referees and uncertain IDs. Expanded footage exposed source-label inconsistencies, including team/role changes under one publisher ID and a potential duplicate jersey identity. Those candidates were excluded without modifying publisher GT. Independent human confirmation of every view remains pending. Match/team separation does not prove roster-level real-person separation across historical games.

## Frozen new test

Weights, configuration, gallery and calibration were locked before downloading Chicago–Toronto frames 901–1500. Sampled 40 query images produced 157 enrolled-player queries and 162 unenrolled-personnel queries. Publisher boxes were used to isolate identity matching: this is oracle-crop retrieval, not end-to-end tracking.

With up to 12 enrollment views, original DINOv2 matched 61/157 known queries (38.9%), with 77 same-team errors. The trained head matched 40/157 (25.5%), with 98 same-team errors. All six tested variants abstained on every query at their frozen calibrated thresholds. No readiness claim; neither trained model is enabled in V6.

## Saved deliverables

Local v13 and v14 contain weights, reproducible training code, fixed protocols, source/model hashes, crop manifests, training history, calibration and per-query CSVs. Six tests passed per experiment. v14/review\_labels.html exposes all 1,759 training/calibration crops and exports a separate review overlay; every identity starts pending. v14/[QA.md](http://QA.md) and review.html summarize results.

Raw video, crops and coordinates are not uploaded here. Test players are not identified as Matvey. V10 archive and original sources remain unchanged; overnight automation remains paused. Next essential work is reviewed identity data and broader training validation, not threshold relaxation.
