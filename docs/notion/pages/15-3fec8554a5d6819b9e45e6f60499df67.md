## ICE-SK-01 — Red-light stop-and-go

- Category: on-ice; target: first steps; controlled stop; 8U starter template.
- Equipment: skates, cones.
- Steps: Skate on green, stop on red, change direction on blue.
- Starting dose: 3x45s; 45s rest; reduce or skip when tired; not an extra obligation.
- Related video rules: SK01,SK02.
- Observable progress: clean stop and immediate balanced restart. Do not infer game transfer from drill performance alone.
- Fun variation: race the traffic lights.
- Progression: slightly increase complexity or decision options only after comfortable execution; regression: slow down, simplify space, remove defender.
- Safety: adult supervision and safe playing area; on-ice activities require appropriate coach/ice allocation; no checking; stop for pain.
- Evidence status: coaching design proposal requiring qualified youth-coach review; not claimed as validated by an individual book.

## Cross-cutting recommendations

Prioritize multi-sport and unstructured play. Never add all drills in a week; select 1–2 priorities and 2–4 small playful activities total only when recovery allows. Keep an option to do nothing structured. Treat performance metrics as observations, not a ranking or psychological label.
