Date 2026-10-19. Same authorized 14.118s MOV, 848×464, 424 frames, \~30 fps.

## Actual implementation

- Selected a clearly visible red skater at 3.0s near pixel (195,299). RED\_TEST\_01 is not the selected player.
- OpenCV Shi–Tomasi feature selection on a local skater ROI, pyramidal Lucas–Kanade forward/backward optical flow, robust median translation with outlier rejection, motion gate, local red torso color sanity check.
- No nearest-neighbor reassignment, no automatic re-identification. When track cannot be supported, mark LOST and stop highlighting rather than switch to a nearby skater.
- Artifacts: /mnt/data/red\_track\_v3\_[conservative.mp](http://conservative.mp)4, /mnt/data/red\_track\_v3\_log.csv, /mnt/data/v3\_qa\_contact.jpg, /mnt/data/track\_[v3.py](http://v3.py).

## Measured state counts

- NOT\_STARTED 90 frames (0–3s).
- TRACKING 201 frames (3.0–9.67s; 6.7 seconds).
- LOST 133 frames (9.69–14.12s).
- These are algorithm state counts, not ground-truth identity accuracy. Sampled QA at 3,4,5,6,7,8,9,9.7,11,13s; approximate marker appears to follow same red skater across much of 3–9s, but this is not a frame-by-frame identity certification.

## Known limitations

- No full 14-second continuous track. No recovery after loss; thus no complete trajectory.
- Optical flow may drift from player to background or another player, and local red patch is not a sufficient identity proof.
- No jersey OCR, object detection, global multi-object tracker, human-labeled ground truth, or puck/tactical analysis.
- Rendering proof, not validated hockey analytics. Original audio not preserved in OpenCV export.

## Next acceptance tests

1. Annotate target skater location and identity every 0.5s from 3–14s, including unknown/occluded segments, as human ground truth.
2. Compute center error and track continuity against ground truth; log false switches and missing intervals.
3. Add player-shaped detector and tracker with Kalman prediction + association gating and appearance cues, with re-ID only when independently supported.
4. Test on crossings and visually similar jerseys; false switches must be zero in release samples, otherwise abstain.
5. Only after stable tracking, attempt a timestamped tactical observation with puck visibility caveats.
